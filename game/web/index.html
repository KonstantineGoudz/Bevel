Controls

Click a binding, then press a key, a mouse button, or a gamepad button… Esc cancels. Left stick moves, right stick orbits, the wheel zooms. The A/B/C/X/Y/Z touch buttons are remapped in the Touch buttons section below (tap Run/Slide to toggle them in game).
